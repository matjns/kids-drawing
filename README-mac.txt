Draw With Me for Mac

This copy is not signed. macOS may say it cannot be opened. That is Gatekeeper,
not a problem with the drawing book. The app does not use the network.

1. Move "Draw With Me.app" to your Applications folder.
2. Control-click the app and choose Open, then Open again.
   Or open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

If it is still blocked, open Terminal and run:

  xattr -cr "/Applications/Draw With Me.app"

Then open the app again.

Closing the window quits Draw With Me. Command-Q quits it too.
The Kids Computer launcher can start it by the bundle id com.kidscomputer.drawing.
